TRAINING PLAN

Lift. Recover. Grow.

Evidence-informed 4-day upper/lower muscle-growth plan, prioritizing supported machines and no squats, deadlifts, bent-over rows or heavy spinal loading. No exercise is completely risk-free: stop or modify anything that aggravates your back.

Start with 2 sets per exercise if returning to training, progressing toward the listed sets as tolerated. Use 1–3 repetitions in reserve and rest roughly 2 minutes between demanding sets. Seek medical or physiotherapy assessment if back pain is persistent, radiating, or accompanied by weakness or numbness.

Workout

~45–65 min

How to progress

Use weights that leave about 2–3 reps in reserve at first. When all sets reach the top of the rep range with good form, increase the weight modestly. Rest about 2–3 minutes for larger lifts and 1–2 minutes for isolation lifts. Prioritize steady progress without provoking back pain.

YOUR PROFILE

Make it yours.

Photo nutrition AI connection

Automatic calorie estimation requires a separately deployed secure AI backend. The app never places your AI provider secret key in public GitHub code. Until a backend is configured, you can still photograph food and log corrected estimates manually.

Photos are transmitted only when you select Analyze. Your backend may send them to an AI provider according to its privacy policies. Protect the endpoint with an access token and spending limits. No secrets are stored in this repository.

Your starting point

For men, the calorie estimate uses the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and an activity multiplier. The result is an approximation, not a measurement of your metabolism.

Backup and privacy

Your data stays in this browser's local storage. It does not sync automatically across devices. Export a backup before switching phones or clearing browser data.

Install on your phone

iPhone: Open the published website in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: Open the website in Chrome, tap the menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Installation and offline support require HTTPS hosting; the local downloaded file can be opened for a preview, but browser behavior may differ.
